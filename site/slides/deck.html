6 · Limits and roadmap

What we did not build, and why.

Limits

  • Data: synthetic, Spanish only, three countries. Portuguese cases are model-written.
  • Ceiling: the policy lets 16 of 56 simulated cases resolve (Simulation). The system resolves all of them. It resolves none beyond them.
  • Mocks: Gold store, login and session, advisor, case store and policy values. Each has a production replacement.
  • Numbers: no number here is a production measurement.

Roadmap

  • Customer 360: the balance has no usable as-of date.
  • Charge investigation: needs real bank data.
  • Feedback dataset: outcomes of advisor handoffs.
  • Spending assistant: needs balances and history.
  • Policy retrieval: needs the bank policy documents.
  • Handoff routing: by language and specialty.
