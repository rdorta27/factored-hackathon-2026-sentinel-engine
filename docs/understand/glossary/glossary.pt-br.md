# Glossário — PT-BR

Brazil overlay on the [canonical English glossary](glossary.en-us.md). The local term stays intact; the explanation is in English for the submission. Most likely language of the Portuguese test cases.

## Banking terms

| Concept | Brazilian term (PT-BR) | English |
|---|---|---|
| Currency | BRL (real) | Brazilian real |
| ID documents | CPF (*Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas*, person registry number) and RG (*carteira de identidade*, identity card) | Person registry number and identity card |
| Checking account | Conta corrente | Checking account |
| Savings account | Conta poupança | Savings account |
| Statement | Extrato | Statement |
| Balance | Saldo | Balance |
| Credit / debit card | Cartão de crédito / débito | Credit / debit card |
| Block the card | Bloquear o cartão | Block the card |
| Unrecognized charge | Compra não reconhecida | Unrecognized purchase |
| Chargeback / refund | Contestação de compra, estorno (refund) | Purchase dispute, refund |
| Pre-authorization | Pré-autorização | Pre-authorization |
| Overdue | Inadimplência, dias de atraso | Delinquency, days past due |
| Instant payment | Pix (Brazil's instant-payment system) | Pix instant payment |

## Customer support

| Concept | Brazilian term (PT-BR) | English |
|---|---|---|
| Call-center agent | Atendente | Agent |
| Support center | Central de atendimento, SAC (*Serviço de Atendimento ao Consumidor*) | Support center, consumer support service |
| Complaints system | SAC; if unresolved, Ouvidoria (the bank's internal ombudsman) | Support service, then internal ombudsman |
| Customer advocate | Ouvidoria | Internal ombudsman |
| Banking regulator | Banco Central do Brasil | Central Bank of Brazil |
| "I want to talk to a person" | Quero falar com um atendente | I want to talk to an agent |
